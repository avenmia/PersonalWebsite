# Personal Website

Michael Avendano's personal site: a public, self-maintained record of his work history and projects. It is the fuller record; the Resume is a deliberately trimmed one-page version of the same underlying facts.

## Language

**Organization**:
A company or group Michael has worked with. The canonical names are **Pangaea Information Technologies** and **Code With Aloha**.
_Avoid_: Pangaea Information Technology (singular), Code for Hawaii, employer, company

**Position**:
A named role held at one Organization over a date range, carrying its own accomplishments.
_Avoid_: job, role, gig, experience

**Professional Experience**:
Positions held for compensation. Kept distinct from Volunteer Experience even though the two are described the same way.
_Avoid_: work history, employment

**Volunteer Experience**:
Positions held without compensation. Kept distinct from Professional Experience even though the two are described the same way.
_Avoid_: side work, unpaid work, community work

**Project**:
A discrete piece of work with its own name, description, and technologies — for example HIERR or the Hawai&#x02bb;i Zoning Atlas. A Project may have been produced within a Position, and may be publicly reachable at a live URL, a source repository, or both.
_Avoid_: app, product, initiative, portfolio piece

**Source Repository**:
The public code repository for a Project. Not every Project has one — client work may be closed-source.
_Avoid_: repo link, GitHub link

**Featured Link**:
A single labelled outbound reference on a Project pointing somewhere other than its Source Repository. It carries its own label because the destination varies in kind: it may be the Project's own live site, or a separate body of work the Project fed into — the Hawai&#x02bb;i Zoning Atlas has no live site of its own but populated the National Zoning Atlas.
_Avoid_: live URL, demo, homepage, project site

**Resume**:
The single-page PDF summary of Michael's career, authored and maintained separately from the site. It is a subset of the site's content, never the source of it.
_Avoid_: CV, resume data
